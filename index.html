What is Phishing?

Phishing is a type of social engineering attack in which attackers try to trick people into revealing sensitive information or taking an unsafe action.

Common Types of Phishing

Email Phishing

Fraudulent emails are designed to look like messages from legitimate organizations.

Fake Login Pages

Attackers may create fake login pages that imitate legitimate websites.

Fraudulent Websites

Fake websites may imitate trusted organizations to trick users.

Social Engineering

Attackers use psychological manipulation to encourage people to reveal information or perform an action.

Warning Signs of Phishing

  • Unexpected messages asking for sensitive information
  • Urgent or threatening language
  • Suspicious links or unfamiliar domains
  • Unexpected attachments
  • Requests for passwords or verification codes
  • Messages containing unusual spelling or grammar

How to Stay Safe

  • Check the sender carefully.
  • Don't click suspicious links.
  • Verify requests through an official channel.
  • Use strong, unique passwords.
  • Enable multi-factor authentication when available.
  • Keep your software and browser updated.
  • Report suspicious messages to the appropriate organization.

Example Scenario

Imagine receiving an unexpected message claiming that your account requires immediate verification.

Instead of clicking the message's link, independently open the organization's official website or contact the organization through a trusted method.
